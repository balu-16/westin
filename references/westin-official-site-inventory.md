The current site sitemap contained 41 ordinary pages, 31 post URLs and 18 event URLs at review. Two post URLs could not be read reliably: `/post/diploma-hotel-management-vijayawada` and `/post/hotel-management-colleges-in-andhra-pradesh`. They were not given invented detail pages. Duplicate navigation links, blank galleries and template text were collapsed. The “Table Magazine 2” menu link leads to a psychometric-test form, so it is not presented as a publication PDF.

The current Home supplied ten broader-industry marks; the current hospitality page supplied 22 hospitality marks. Sixteen additional distinct named organizations appear in the placement and news material. `Student_portal/src/public/OfficialHighlights.tsx` lists each name and its source. The strips say “Companies featured by Westin.” A mark or name does not by itself prove a hiring arrangement, partnership, salary or individual student result.

Images under `Student_portal/public/images/official/` were derived from the current Westin pages: two supporting photos and 18 event-gallery photos. Company marks under `Student_portal/public/images/companies/` were derived from Westin’s published strips. The illustrated Home and public-page heroes remain the main visual system. Visible image captions remain hidden by request; alt text identifies actual college photos and conceptual art accurately.

The source-linked content model is in `Student_portal/src/public/content.ts` and `officialArchive.ts`. Locally migrated pages work without the API. A build-time snapshot of published CMS entries is optional and described in `Student_portal/README.md`.
